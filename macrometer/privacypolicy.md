**Last Updated: March 27, 2026**

Macrometer is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data, specifically regarding the collection, retention, and deletion of information.

---

### 1. Collection and Use of Health Data
Macrometer is a calorie-tracking application. To provide its core functionality, the app processes **Health Data**, specifically:

* **Nutrition Data:** This includes food items, calorie counts, and macronutrient information manually entered by the user.
* **Usage:** This data is used solely to provide local tracking, visual progress charts, and nutritional analysis within the app.

**Storage:** This data is stored locally on your device. No nutrition or personal health data ever leaves your device, and no personal health information is collected or accessed by the developer.

### 2. Third-Party Data Collection (Crash Reporting)
We use **Firebase Crashlytics** (a service provided by Google) to collect crash reports and improve app stability. If Macrometer crashes, it sends the following information to Crashlytics:
* Device type, OS version, and hardware information.
* The state of the application at the time of the crash and stack traces.

This information does not include your IP address, name, or any health/nutrition data. It cannot be used to identify you or your mobile device individually.

### 3. Data Retention
Because Macrometer operates entirely on your device without a server-side component:
* **Health and Nutrition Data** is retained on your device for as long as the app is installed or until you manually clear the app data.
* **Crashlytics data** is retained by Firebase according to their standard retention and privacy policies.

### 4. Data Deletion
You have full control over your data. You can delete all health and nutrition data at any time through the following methods:
1.  **System Settings:** Navigate to Android System Settings > Apps > Macrometer > Storage & Cache and select **"Clear Storage"** or **"Clear Data"**.
2.  **Uninstallation:** Uninstalling the Macrometer app from your device will permanently delete all locally stored health and nutrition data.
